- Director
- Cast
- Country
- Date Added
- Release Year
- Rating
- Duration
- Genres

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Netflix content increased rapidly after **2015**
- **Movies dominate** the platform compared to TV Shows
- **United States and India** contribute a large share of content
- **Drama and international genres** are among the most common
